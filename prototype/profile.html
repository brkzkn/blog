Technical focus

What I reach for, and what I am careful with.

Architecture

Domain-driven design, bounded contexts, event-driven integration, CQRS where the read side earns it, strangler migrations off monoliths.

Backend

.NET, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, gRPC and REST. Strong opinions about idempotency and the outbox pattern.

Platform

Kubernetes, GitOps, OpenTelemetry. Golden paths that make the compliant route the easy one.

Working with AI

Provider-independent application design, prompt versioning, eval datasets. Interested in what a small local model can do reliably.

Away from the keyboard

I grew up in Istanbul and still live on the Anatolian side. Football is a lifelong thing: I follow the amateur leagues as closely as the professional ones, and I am a congress member at Fenerbahçe, which means I have opinions nobody asked for.

At home there is a workshop corner, a printer cabinet I designed and built, and a house that is slowly learning to do the right thing without being asked. The Life section is where I keep the rest: trips, books, what we watched, and the days worth writing down.
