How I draw a system

Four layers, two kinds of lines, and a legend that fits on one line. Solid lines are requests, dashed lines are events. If I cannot say which one a line is, the diagram is not finished.

What a case study here covers

Business context. Who needed what, and why now.

The architectural problem. The thing the current system could not do.

Constraints. Teams, budget, legacy, regulation, release cadence.

System boundaries. Who owns which data and which events.

Retail, 2024 to 2025

Rebuilding order orchestration for a multi-brand retailer

Splitting a shared order monolith into domain services with an event log at the centre, while four brands kept shipping daily.

KafkaOutboxSagasPostgreSQLKubernetes

Decision

Event log as the integration contract, not shared tables

Trade-off

Eventual consistency in stock; a reconciliation job to catch drift

Outcome

Independent deploys per domain; [metric] fewer stuck orders

Lesson

Draw the boundaries around who may change a rule, not around tables
