## Summary

Museums at a slow pace, one long walk along the river, Borough Market twice, and the best flat white of the year. Full itinerary, notes and what we would change.

## Facts

- Stayed | Southwark, near Borough
- Travelling | Two adults, twins at eight months

## Day by day

Times are rough. Everything bent around naps.

### 2025-03-14 | Arrive, walk, eat

- 13:00 | Heathrow to Southwark on the Elizabeth line. Pram-friendly, surprisingly.
- 16:00 | Walk along the river from the Globe to Tate Modern. Turbine Hall as a running track for us, not them.
- 18:30 | Early dinner at Borough Market, Padella (queue, worth it).

@places Southbank, Tate Modern, Borough Market

### 2025-03-15 | Museums at toddler speed

- 09:30 | Natural History Museum before the crowds. The blue whale is the whole visit for an eight-month-old.
- 12:30 | Lunch and a nap in Hyde Park; coffee at the Serpentine.
- 16:00 | V&A, two galleries only, then the courtyard.

@places South Kensington, Hyde Park, V&A

### 2025-03-16 | Neighbourhoods, then home

- 09:00 | Columbia Road flower market, then breakfast on Redchurch Street.
- 11:30 | Walk through Shoreditch to Spitalfields. Bought nothing, photographed everything.
- 15:00 | Back to the hotel for bags, Elizabeth line to Heathrow.

## Practical notes

- Elizabeth line from Heathrow beats a taxi with a double pram, and most stations have lifts.
- Big museums open at 10:00; be there at 9:50. By 11:30 the pram is a liability.
- Borough Market on Friday evening is calmer than Saturday noon.
- Pharmacies close early on Sunday. Pack what you need.
- Contactless card everywhere; we never used cash.

## Favourite moments

The Turbine Hall at Tate Modern, empty on a Friday afternoon, and realising neither of them had ever seen a room that big. The flower market at 9 a.m., all four of us in the same mood for once. And the flat white on Redchurch Street, which I am still thinking about.

## What I would change next time

- One museum per day, not two.
- Book Padella-style places for 17:30 instead of queueing.
- Stay four nights. Day three was the first day we felt like we lived there.
